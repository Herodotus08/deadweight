🖐 Grab and fling them. Damage comes from the impact — slam them into the dirt, spike them, or bowl them into each other.
🩹 A zombie that survives a throw will get back up. Finish it.
💪 Big ones fight your grip. Heavies and bosses need a grip level just to try, and even then you may slip — keep wrestling, every failed attempt makes the next one likelier.
🛡 Armour only stops gadgets, never your hands. The answer to an armoured zombie is always a throw.
💣 Pick a gadget off the belt, then tap the field to use it.
🏚 Keep them off the shack. Kills buy upgrades between waves.
